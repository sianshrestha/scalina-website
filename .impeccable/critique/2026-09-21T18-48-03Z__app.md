## Design Specificity Verdict

**Partly authored.**

The motion vocabulary, the ground engine and the type voice are Scalina's and nobody else's: the Oswald-700 hero at 302px, the four-ground token engine, the pinned `ColorWipe` cutting letters mid-glyph, the `/about` circle wipe, the Attract→Amplify→Convert→Operate card stack, the Media-light/Systems-dark counterweight. That is a real idea about a real company with two halves.

The moment the page has to carry **evidence** rather than assertion, it becomes any agency template. `SelectedWork`'s entire accessible text is `"LATEST WORK | See all work | →"` — nine unlabelled gradient rectangles. `Shipped` spends 3,824px on five navy panels captioned "screen capture · 16:10". ## Overall Impression

This is a site with an unusually good engine and an unusually empty tank. The scroll performance is genuinely excellent — **60fps with zero long frames** across five separate animated regions including a 4.2-viewport sticky column, two pinned wipes and a four-card pinned stack. The focus ring survives a colour-shifting scroll, which most sites like this lose. The writing, where it exists, is the best thing here.

And then you scroll 12,000px to find out whether this agency can build anything, and you are shown coloured rectangles.

**The single biggest opportunity is not a design change — it is putting the work on the work page.** Everything else on this list is a refinement. That one is the argument.

## What's Working

1. **The ground-token engine is real engineering.** `lib/grounds.ts` interpolates surfaces, *steps* foregrounds, and derives the step point from live contrast maths rather than a hardcoded percentage. Verified by tabbing: the focus ring renders `2px solid #1E45FB` at 5.7:1 on light and lime at 15.6:1 on dark. Most colour-shifting sites lose the ring on one ground entirely.

2. **Scroll performance under heavy scrubbing is flawless.** Zero long frames across Hero→Teams, the 3,500px `Shipped` sticky, the tagline wipe, the pinned stage stack and the `SelectedWork` parallax. That is the direct payoff of the recorded decisions: no Lenis, `Reveal` never using `once: true`, `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` deferred past late-sizing sections.

3. **`prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured in 37 files**, including every vendored component — and the vendored React Bits copies were *fixed* rather than shipped as-is (upstream `ScrollReveal` kills every ScrollTrigger on unmount, which would have destroyed the page's whole motion system).

**What:** `/services?view=systems` → `ServiceProof` renders testimonial cards for **"Atlas Freight" @atlasfreight** and **"Verity Logistics" @veritylogistics**, plus Northbridge Dental and Harper & Co (`lib/services.ts:147,153,238,244`). Three sourced numbers beat six numbers and four fictional companies. Attach a source to every numeral in `Teams`.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] `/work` has 1,203px of horizontal overflow at desktop width

Traced by descending scrollWidth to `div.swiper.swiper-cards` (scrollWidth 2,124 / clientWidth 400) inside `components/vendor/Carousel.module.css:24`, which sets `overflow: visible` on a `clamp(260px, 30vw, 400px)` deck so the cards-effect stack escapes its box. **Fix:** `overflow: clip` on `.swiperCards` with `overflow-clip-margin: 40px` so the card shadows still breathe, or move the deck into a `overflow-x: clip` wrapper at shell width. Verify `maxScrollX === 0` at 1440 and 1920 after.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The work does not appear anywhere on the site

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] The fixed header prints on top of body copy on three of five routes
**What:** `SiteHeader.module.css` `.header` computes `background-color: rgba(0,0,0,0)` — no scrim, no blur, no gradient — while `main` scrolls under it. The "SCALINA" wordmark was captured sitting on top of `WhatWeDo`'s lede, the `ScalinaSystem` statement, the founder quote and `AboutProof`'s heading.
**Why it matters:** Two pieces of text at the same weight and colour on top of each other is the most basic legibility failure there is.
**Fix:** `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(1.1)` plus `background: color-mix(in srgb, var(--bg) 72%, transparent)` and `transition: background 320ms var(--ease-standard)`, fully transparent only while `scrollY < 40`. If chrome is unacceptable, put a 70px `mask-image` fade on the sections beneath instead — but transparent-with-no-treatment cannot stay.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

### [P2] The declared type and spacing scales are not enforced, and it now shows
**What:** 36 font sizes, 31 tracking values, 49 spacing values (6 on scale) on one page. The label band alone runs **eight** sizes — 10.56, 10.88, 11.2, 11.52, 11.84, 12.16, 12.48, 12.8 — where the system specifies one 11px eyebrow. The same FlowingMenu label renders at both 14.69px and 14.72px. `WhatWeDo`'s `<h2>` is 64px Oswald at `line-height: normal` (≈1.48) rather than the 0.96 §2 specifies, and is not on the `.sectionHeading` utility.
**Why it matters:** This is why the page reads slightly unresolved despite each section being well composed — no two gaps are the same number, so nothing lines up with anything.
**Fix:** Add `--s-4 … --s-128` and `--t-eyebrow/small/body/statement/card/section` token blocks to `:root`. Replace every spacing `clamp()` with a two-step `clamp(var(--s-N), Xvw, var(--s-M))` so both ends land on the scale. Pin every eyebrow to `var(--t-eyebrow)` and `0.2em` — one, not eight. There is a `test:contrast`; add a `test:scale` that fails the build on an off-scale computed gap.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset` then `$impeccable layout`
